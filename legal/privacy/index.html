Privacy policy

Who we are

Field Ready QHSE is the controller responsible for the personal data described here. Seller details are on our seller information page. Contact: fieldready.qhse@outlook.com.

What we collect

  • When you buy: Gumroad collects your name, email, billing country and payment details as merchant of record, and shares your email and order details with us so we can support your order.
  • When you use the Course Room (video courses): we check your email address and licence key with Gumroad when you sign in, but do not store them. The Course Room keeps your lesson progress, quiz attempts and answers, and certificate under a one-way code made from your licence key, plus the name you type for your certificate. For access codes we store only one-way codes of the code and your email address. To stop people guessing keys, it counts failed sign-ins per internet address per day. It sets one strictly necessary sign-in cookie (7 days) and saves your progress in your browser. Anyone with your certificate number can see the name, course, date and score on the certificate check page.
  • When you email us: your email address and message.

What we don't do

This website sets no advertising or tracking cookies, loads its fonts from our own server and does not sell your data. When you open the Gumroad checkout, Gumroad's own privacy and cookie policy applies.

How long we keep it

Order records are kept as long as tax law requires. Email enquiries are kept for 24 months. Course Room records are kept while the course is available to you, so you can come back to it and so certificates can be checked; ask us and we delete them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct, delete or export your data, or object to how we use it. Email fieldready.qhse@outlook.com. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Portugal: CNPD).
