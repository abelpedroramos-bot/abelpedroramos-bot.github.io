Video course help

Our video courses live in the Course Room: watch the lessons, take the final quiz and get your certificate of completion. This page explains every step.

Quick start: Open the Course Room (frq-course-room.fieldreadyqhse.workers.dev). Enter the email address you bought with and the licence key from your Gumroad receipt, then press Open my course. No password and no account to create.

Open your course

  1. Open the Course Room. Go to frq-course-room.fieldreadyqhse.workers.dev or use the Course Room link in your Gumroad receipt.
  2. Type the email address you used at checkout.
  3. Paste your licence key (four blocks of 8 letters and numbers, for example XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX-XXXXXXXX).
  4. Press Open my course. Your courses appear under My courses.

You stay signed in on that device for 7 days. On a shared or site-office computer, use Sign out when you finish.

More than one course? Each course has its own licence key. Sign in with one key, then add the others from My courses. If you bought the course bundle, one key opens every course in it.

Where to find your licence key

  • Your Gumroad receipt email. Straight after payment Gumroad emails your receipt. The licence key is shown in it. Search your inbox (and spam folder) for “Gumroad” and the course name.
  • Your Gumroad library. Go to gumroad.com/library and sign in with the email you bought with. Open the course: the licence key is shown on its page. If you have no Gumroad account yet, create one with the same email address and your purchases appear in the library.

Copy and paste the key rather than typing it. A key only opens the course it was sold with.

Bought before 25 November 2026?

Courses bought before 25 November 2026 were sold without a licence key. Ask us for an access code using the button below. We check your purchase in Gumroad and reply by email with a code like FRQ-A-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX.

Enter the code in the Licence key box, together with the email address you bought with. The code works only with that email address.

Ask for an access code

Watching the lessons

  • Lessons are in order on the left (on a phone: below the video). A lesson gets its tick when you have played at least 90% of it. Skipping ahead does not count.
  • Lessons marked optional are extras and are not needed for the quiz.
  • Your progress is saved on your device and in the Course Room, so you can stop at any time and continue on another device. A lesson you left part way through resumes where you stopped, with a link to start from the beginning.

Low data mode and downloads

  • Above each video you can switch between HD and Low data. Low data plays a smaller copy of the video that uses about a quarter of the mobile data. It is ideal on site, on a phone hotspot or abroad.
  • The Course Room picks Low data for you when your phone or browser reports a slow connection or data saver is switched on. You can change it at any time.
  • Each lesson can also be downloaded to keep for offline viewing and toolbox use. Downloaded videos do not tick lessons: to count towards the quiz, a lesson must be played in the Course Room.
  • Subtitles (English captions) can be switched on in the player.

Final quiz rules

  • The quiz opens when every required lesson has its tick.
  • 15 questions, multiple choice. You need 12 correct (80%) to pass.
  • You have 2 attempts. Your answers are marked when you submit.
  • If you don’t pass the first time, the Course Room shows which questions were wrong (not the right answers), so you can re-watch those lessons and try again.
  • When you pass, you see the explanation for every question.
  • Used both attempts without passing? Re-watch the lessons, then ask us to reset your quiz with the button below. Please give your licence key (or access code) so we can find your record.

Ask for a quiz reset

Your certificate

  • After you pass, open Certificate of completion on the course page.
  • Type your name exactly as it should appear, for example for your employer or the site’s training records. Check the spelling: the name cannot be changed once the certificate is issued.
  • Download the certificate as a PDF. You can download it again from the course page at any time.
  • Each certificate has its own certificate number (for example FRQ-SI-2026-XXXX-XXXX) and a check link printed on it.

The certificate records that you completed the Field Ready QHSE course and passed its final quiz. It is not a regulated qualification, a licence or a site card, and it does not replace the site-specific induction your site gives you.

Check a certificate

Employers, site managers and auditors can check any certificate on the certificate check page (frq-course-room.fieldreadyqhse.workers.dev/verify/). The check link printed on each certificate opens it with the number already filled in. Enter the certificate number (the last two blocks never use the characters O, 0, I or 1, so they can’t be mixed up). For a valid certificate the page shows the name, course, date of issue and final quiz score. Otherwise it says that no valid certificate was found, for example after a typing error or when a certificate was withdrawn after a refund. No other details about the learner are shown.

Sign-in problems

  • “We could not find that licence key”: copy the key again from your receipt email or Gumroad library and paste it with no spaces before or after it. Pre-25-November buyers: use the access code we sent you.
  • “This licence key was bought with a different email address”: use the email address on your Gumroad receipt, not a work alias or another address.
  • “This purchase was refunded”: a refunded course can no longer be opened.
  • “We could not check your key just now”: the key check service is busy. Wait a few minutes and try again.
  • “Too many attempts today”: after many failed sign-ins from one connection, sign-in pauses until the next day. This protects your key from guessing.
  • Video won’t play: refresh the page (video links renew themselves), switch to Low data or try another browser. Company networks sometimes block video: try your phone’s mobile data.
