What's inside

  • 15 toolbox talks (talks 46-60): line of fire and pinch points, hand safety and gloves, eye protection, openings holes and fragile surfaces, mobile access towers, fall arrest anchors and rescue, slinging and signalling, forklifts and telehandlers, compact plant (dumpers and skid steers), getting on and off plant, carbon monoxide and engine-driven equipment, biological hazards (sewage, rats and sharps), gas cylinders and compressed air, protecting the public and road works, last-minute risk check when the job changes
  • Each talk has two pages. The worker sheet has the message, good and bad practice photos, five key messages, the before-you-start checks and the stop trigger
  • The speaker sheet has the timings, talking points, a practical insight, a safe demonstration, three questions with answers, 'If someone asks' and the references
  • A reusable Attendance and Actions record
  • A supervisor preparation page
  • A reference register with clickable HSE and NIOSH links
